# Viral Ground Product Context

## Platform

- Responsive web application with public marketing pages and authenticated role workspaces.
- Primary language is Korean, with English localization available where the current app supports it.

## Users

- Company operators who create and manage AI SaaS creator campaigns.
- Creators who discover campaigns, apply, submit content, and record performance.
- Administrators who oversee the managed-beta operation.

## Core Purpose

Viral Ground connects AI SaaS brands with creators and gives both sides one managed workflow from campaign discovery and applications through content submission and performance recording.

## Positioning

- AI SaaS-focused creator network, not a general influencer marketplace.
- Managed beta: operational transparency and reliable handoffs matter more than inflated marketplace claims.
- Role dashboards are working surfaces for the next action, not decorative analytics pages.

## Confirmed Capabilities

- Role-based authentication for company, creator, and admin users.
- Company campaign creation and management.
- Creator campaign discovery, applications, content submission, and performance entry.
- Direct Meta Instagram connection workflow.
- Public creator profiles are opt-in.
- Object storage supports secure upload completion and ownership checks.

## Operational Constraints

- Live payment and settlement are not enabled until a production payment provider and operating contract are configured.
- Payment-related UI must fail closed and must never instruct users to transfer funds to placeholder accounts.
- Dashboard metrics must come from existing APIs or display an explicit zero, empty, or preparation state.
- Legal document versions and production environment values are deployment responsibilities.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Viral Ground.
- The established paper, ink, and electric-violet palette is a durable brand asset.
- Archivo Black is reserved for high-impact English display copy; Korean body and interface copy use the established Korean sans-serif stack.
- The product should feel editorial, direct, and operational rather than like a generic SaaS template.

## Evidence Boundary

- No verified aggregate performance, revenue, or marketplace-size claims are currently available for dashboard decoration.
- Sample media must be identified as sample content until usage rights and real campaign provenance are confirmed.
- Financial states shown from legacy or managed records must be labeled accurately and must not imply live automated settlement.

## Product Principles

1. Show the next useful action before secondary analytics.
2. Separate company and creator responsibilities while keeping one recognizable system.
3. Prefer truthful empty states over invented activity.
4. Keep sensitive files, contact details, and financial records within their authorized role boundary.
5. Preserve the paper–ink–violet identity across authenticated and public experiences.
